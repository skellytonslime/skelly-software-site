AllotBot!

A Chrome extension side panel that sits alongside your dispensary POS. One click verifies a patient's remaining MMP allotment against the Connecticut PMP — without leaving the patient profile, without slowing down the transaction.

ALLOTBOT!
CT PMP Allotment Verification · Chrome Extension

AllotBot! integrates with Connecticut's prescription monitoring program to calculate a patient's internal and external dispensations, remaining allotment, and 30-day window dates — displayed instantly in the browser alongside the dispensary workflow.

  • Instant allotment calculation — internal vs. external breakdown
  • Estimated cycle date with confidence rating
  • Physician's Instructions lookup via CT Biznet
  • One-click allotment sync to Dutchie POS profile
  • Stateless — zero patient data stored, ever
  • Licensed per dispensary location

About

Built by a pharmacist, for the whole team.

Skelly Software was founded by a licensed pharmacist and Medical Program Manager with nearly a decade of experience in Connecticut's regulated cannabis industry.

Why we exist

Cannabis dispensaries need compliance tools that work for everyone on the floor. We build software that reduces manual overhead, catches errors at the point of sale, and keeps teams operating within regulatory requirements without slowing them down.

Where we're headed

AllotBot! is actively deployed at Fine Fettle dispensaries across Connecticut. We're expanding to additional dispensary chains and building new tools to address the compliance and operational gaps that dispensary staff face every shift.
